Notable in 2026

FACT

  • First IEEE conference papers. All 7 listed papers from 2024–2025 were national journal papers.
  • Two papers at one venue, twice: ISIBER (Feb) and SIML (Jun).
  • 11-author papers (INFINITYS, ICITRI). These were our largest collaborations so far.
  • 7 universities in co-author affiliations on the conference decks: Esa Unggul, Brawijaya, Hasanuddin, Terbuka, Jember, Sains Indonesia, Dian Nusantara.
  • Beyond AI: a public-policy paper in JIAPI.

OBSERVATION

  • Retrieval/LLM and medical imaging were the two most frequent 2026 themes.
  • Medical imaging spans all three years: heart attack (2024), brain tumor and diabetes (2025), then malaria, pneumonia, glaucoma and liver (2026).
  • 5 of the 13 pieces of 2026 work are comparative benchmarks rather than new methods.
